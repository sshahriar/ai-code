## Bottom line

How much it speeds things up depends hugely on the kind of task, on the kinds of skills that you have, on the size of the project, and many other factors. But you quickly get to understand what kind of boost you're going to get once you're a little bit into that project. Generally speaking, if you're dealing with a small MVP of a green field project, you're going to get a very significant multiplier of your productivity. In most other cases, you're talking about a speed improvement, you're talking about assistance, but not the kind of 10X numbers that you sometimes see in all of the hype.
